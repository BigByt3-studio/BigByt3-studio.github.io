Paystride Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026
App: Paystride (Android)
Publisher: BrokenStack Studios LLC
Contact: support@brokenstackstudios.com

The short version

Paystride keeps your budget on your phone. There's no account to make, no bank login, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. BrokenStack Studios doesn't receive, store or sell your financial information. We couldn't see it even if we wanted to.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in an encrypted database on your phone. That includes:

  • your paychecks
  • your bills and debts
  • your purchases and receipts
  • your budget categories
  • your settings

That database is only readable by Paystride on that phone.

  • Receipt photos. The camera is used only when you scan a receipt. The text on the receipt is read on your phone and is never uploaded. You can choose to keep the photo with the purchase or have it discarded after saving. Kept photos stay in Paystride's private storage on your phone.
  • Spreadsheet and bank statement imports. Files you choose to import are read on your phone. They're not sent anywhere.
  • Reminders. Bill and payday reminders are scheduled by your phone from your plan. They are not sent from a server.
  • App lock. Fingerprint, face or PIN checks are handled by Android. Paystride never sees or stores your biometric data.
  • Home-screen widget. If you add the widget, it keeps a copy of the three short lines it shows (your safe-to-spend amount and next payday) in Paystride's private storage. Turn on Hide amount on the widget in Settings and it shows only a lock.

Backups

  • Google backup (optional, on by default). If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include Paystride's encrypted database in your Google account backup. On Android 9 and later this backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. It is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, and BrokenStack Studios has no access to it. You can turn it off in Paystride's Settings.
  • Backup files. "Export everything" creates a file that you save or share wherever you choose. That file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

What we collect

Nothing. Paystride has no account system, analytics, advertising SDKs or crash reporting, and doesn't send your data to BrokenStack Studios or anyone else.

If a future version adds an optional feature that needs the internet, this policy will be updated first, and the feature will be off until you turn it on. Examples are linking a bank account through a provider such as Plaid, or anonymous crash reports.

Children

Paystride is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything deletes everything Paystride stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app does the same. Backup files you exported are yours to delete. Google backups follow your Google account's backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
